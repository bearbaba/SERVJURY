## Editing pass

- Tight crops, smooth 200–350ms transitions, subtle zoom on **actual** hashes and verdict fields. Show no huge terminal scroll.
- Animate verdict after its real transaction has already finalized; never imply time-to-finality shorter than observed.
- Record at 1080p or 1440p; use high-contrast subtitles in the lower safe area; avoid unreadable text.
- Use original narration and properly licensed music only, or no music. AI-assisted motion/caption editing as required by mission.
- The final slate must contain **three real logos**, not just three textual brand names. Place real repository URL once published.
- Do not submit an offline-fixture-only recording; it does not demonstrate GenLayer consensus or a real partner call.
